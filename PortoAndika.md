## UI/UX Design Direction

This portfolio uses a **Futuristic / Digital Tech** visual direction.

### Visual identity

- **Background:** deep navy / near-black (`#090D1A`)
- **Primary accent:** neon cyan (`#22D3EE`)
- **Secondary accent:** electric purple (`#8B5CF6`)
- **Text:** soft white (`#E5E7EB`) and muted gray (`#94A3B8`)
- **Surface:** translucent dark panels with subtle borders
- **Typography:** modern sans-serif such as Inter, Space Grotesk, or Sora

### Interface components

- Sticky navigation with clear active states
- Hero section with name, role, short introduction, and primary action
- Profile/about section with readable text
- Skill chips or grouped skill cards
- Project cards with screenshots, descriptions, and clear links
- Education and certificate timeline/cards
- Contact section with direct links
- Footer with social links and copyright

### Interaction and UX

- Navigation links scroll to the relevant section.
- Buttons show hover, focus, and pressed states.
- Animations are subtle and do not block reading.
- Text contrast remains readable against dark backgrounds.
- Links have descriptive labels; icons are not the only way to identify
  actions.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for visitors who prefer less animation.
- Keep content concise and make projects easy to scan.

### Responsive behavior

| Screen | Layout guidance |
| ------ | --------------- |
| Mobile (under 600px) | Single-column layout, compact navigation, full-width buttons, readable spacing |
| Tablet (600--1023px) | Two-column cards where suitable, balanced margins |
| Desktop (1024px and above) | Wider content container, multi-column project/skill grids, spacious hero |

### Suggested page order

1. Home / Hero
2. About
3. Skills
4. Projects
5. Education
6. Certificates
7. Contact
